## Method and negative controls

All ten original case roles remain, with nonempty `raw.varm` added to the toy
fixtures, plus an eleventh case preserving NaN, positive infinity and negative
infinity. These are new small technical fixtures, not claims that historical
fixture files were byte-identical or that frozen study inputs were modified.
The reference independently calls the official AnnData slicing API; the node
runs in a separate Python process. The pinned author's implementation documents
axis-aligned subsetting and materialization of copied views.
[AnnData 0.11.4 source](https://github.com/scverse/anndata/blob/0.11.4/src/anndata/_core/anndata.py)

Each complete output H5AD is byte-equal to its author reference. Comparison also
checks X, raw, layers, both axes, embeddings, variable annotations, observation
and variable graphs, nested metadata, every HDF5 object/attribute/dataset,
explicit shapes/dtypes and strict report contracts. Recorded counters total
1149 finite component checks, 317 HDF5 dataset checks and 66 ledger rows;
these are repeated technical comparisons, not independent biological samples.
Nonfinite entries preserve their positions and signs and are excluded from the
finite counter, not silently converted to valid expression/QC observations.

All 24 original boundary cases execute: 22 specific exit-2 wrapper refusals,
one corrupt-Parquet exit-3 refusal, and one separately controlled ordinary
second-link rollback. OOM or launch failure cannot stand in for these refusals.

Twelve actual comparator controls are archived:

- Five data-corruption controls, including all four original controls and
  changed `raw.varm`, are rejected by both old and new whole-object comparators.
- Five report mutations are actually accepted by the old comparator but
  rejected by the new one: missing unselected ledger rows, wrong byte count,
  extra output receipt, invented donor qualification and nonstandard NaN JSON.
- Scalar broadcasting and a sparse shape mismatch are accepted by the old
  shared helper but rejected by the fixed helper. These two are not represented
  as old whole-HDF5 comparison successes.

The comparison-only changes enforce shapes/types, full ledger and receipt
equality, strict JSON, and explicit authority boundaries. They do not change
the production slicing algorithm. These counterexamples do not demonstrate
that historical real outputs actually contained the mutated reports.
